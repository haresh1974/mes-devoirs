📝 Exam tips for written answers
  • Answer in French, in your own words. Do not copy a whole sentence.
  • Change the person: je → il / elle; mon frère → son frère.
  • A 2-mark question needs two different details.
  • Keep the tense of the question: a question in the passé composé or imparfait is answered in the past.
